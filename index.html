About

I build the hardware inside robots, and more importantly, I keep it running. Four-plus years between research labs and production floors taught me the fun part isn't the first prototype. It's the fifth one that finally stops breaking.

Mechatronics engineer with 4+ years in product development and integration for robotic systems. I work where mechanical design, electronics, sensors, actuators, and controls meet: end-effectors and fixtures, humanoid teleoperation hardware, dexterous hands, AMR fleets, and industrial robot cells. Hands-on from CAD and prototyping through commissioning and root-cause analysis, with experience training technicians to improve reliability and reduce downtime.
